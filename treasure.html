Treasure Battle #1 · Boss

HIBANA QUE

The Blue Current is coming.

In base form he is a calm black-haired shinobi — topknot bound, beard neat, gold-trimmed jacket, the quiet before weather. He fights on the ground like a man. But at his earned peak the storm stops asking permission: SPARK ASCENSION fires — his muscles pop, his hair bursts loose, grows long and wild and turns BLUE, gold lightning crawling through it, and his GOD AURA ignites — the Blue Current, a divine blue flame standing off his whole body while the air and debris around him answer it. The calm man goes in. The storm comes out wearing his face — god-grin and all.

And in TREASURE BATTLE #1 he does not stay on the ground. Sheeted as THE FIRST DOOR: HIBANA QUE fights in the air — the aerial boss — while OGUN, the anvil, holds the ground below him. Iron from beneath. Lightning from above.
