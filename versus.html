How it works

The
Rules

VERSUS is continuous — the wheel never stops. New fighters enter the draw as they are designed, and every drop updates the ledger and this page. The wheel now draws from 26 locked fighters.

The Draw

Two fighters are drawn at random from the locked roster of 26 fighters, in a universe arena drawn at random. No rematches until the wheel turns full. The winner is drawn too — except when THE ARTIST steps in.

The Formula

Every fight is built on THE FORMULA — one locked fight command, three laws in force:

  • Describe the dance, never the contact — impact carries the hit.
  • One beat per clip, stitched in the edit.
  • Impacts-only sound: punches, kicks, clashes. Nothing else.

The Boss Ladder

Win three fights and you earn a BOSS FIGHT against THE ARTIST — Que himself, the final boss of his own universe. The fight is real and the drama is in how he wins… but he never loses. Nobody ever has. The boss rotation is live: the door opens at three wins — and a rare pull can bring THE ARTIST into any draw unannounced.
