About

The man behind the pencil.

Que is a realism pencil artist from Little Rock, Arkansas — and the founder of the Afro-Anime genre. What started as sketchbooks became a universe: anime, manga, kids shows and music, all original, all from his hand and his mind.

His rule for everything here: we create, not steal. Original characters, original worlds, original powers — stories that feel like they came from inside a Black man's mind, because they did.

Guiding it all is MIDAS, the pocket-sized golden god — Que's mascot, riding in his pocket, turning everything gold.
